# Lusus Naturae
## 1.20.1 Forge Modpack - Concept Document
You are a scientist researching the occult against your superiors' orders. Something has gone terribly wrong and now everyone in the facility is dead, aside from you. Anomalies have breached containment, horrors are roaming free, and a special task force is coming to clean up both the hazards and you. The voice pushes you further into the unknown as you will need to commit to further rituals or augment yourself to resist the incoming deluge.

## Core Loop
Explore -> Scavenge Resources -> Craft/Rituals -> Fight/Capture Mobs/Hazards -> Unlock New Areas

## Gameplay Summary
The player explores new rooms and hallways in the facility and finds resources to scavenge that can either be used as-is or can be dismantled into other resources. Threat levels should increase the further the player goes into the facility, encountering anomalies, infections and hostile enemies. The player can capture some of these hostile mobs and turn them into special resources or a summon. Some bosses will drop keycards used to access deeper levels of the facility. 

## Worldgen
Lusus Naturae will make use of a closed world dungeon crawler system. The player will always respawn at their initial office or where they have set their spawn and they can always recover their items after death.

### Procedural Facility
The laboratory underground facility serves as the procedurally generated dungeon in gameplay. It is comprised of high-tech elements, furniture and props which can be scavenged. 

The starting room of the laboratory is always the office, leading into a hallway connected to multiple rooms. These rooms may contain props, hostile mobs, anomalies, etc. The hallway may lead to more hallways or more rooms, and rooms can also lead to more hallways. Further areas of the facility may be corrupted or infected.

### Areas
The facility should be split into specific areas with a set danger level, set resources and set challenges and theming. 

These areas could either be separate dimensions or a grid or ring system into the facility.

#### Gating
To access new areas, a keycard must be obtained which could be obtained with the following methods: quest reward, boss drop, crafting.

## Gameplay & Progression

### Scavenging
Resources can be obtained by scavenging, either found in chests/containers, in scrap piles or as objects which can be dismantled.

A recycler should be present in specific rooms and the office to dismantle objects.

### Crafting
Specific rooms and the office may contain crafting stations, used to turn resources into other resources and useable items.

Crafting can also be conducted via rituals.

### Rituals & Summoncrafting
Rituals can be conducted to serve several purposes such as transmutation and binding. Binding is the main method to capture mobs and turning them into idols. Idols can be dismantled into resources or turned into other mobs. Transmutation can be used to turn resources into other types of resources. Advanced rituals can also bind a powerful item to the player, create a semi-safe zone and produce a familiar.

The rituals described above are components of the mod 'Pact Magic', among others.

### Augmentations
The player can augment themselves with technology to improve themselves. This may require special resources to be found such as rarer loot or parts obtained from captured mobs.

This concept may be scrapped if it is too hard to implement.

### Threats
Various threats will be found in different areas of the facility.

#### Mobs
Regular mobs may be found such as vanilla mobs and simple enemies with melee or ranged attack patterns. They can either be defeated regularly or captured.

In later areas of the facility, task force troops wielding firearms may be encountered.

#### Anomalies
Anomalies can be found in specific rooms of the facility, providing special mechanics, challenges and an awe factor. 

The anomalies described above are components of the mod 'Strange Matter', among others.

#### Bosses
Bossses can be found in specific rooms of the facility and may be a method to obtain keycards to progress further.

## Misc Concepts

### Research Rewards
The player may receive research missions from either 'The Voice' or remotely via the unknown 'Investor'. These missions will provide knowledge and item and financial rewards to the player.

These research rewards may be implemented as quests in 'FTBQuests'.

### Economy System
The player may interact with a remote shopping station with an embedded teleporter, allowing them to sell and purchase items with the outside world. These materials could be ones not found within the facility or be an alternate way to acquire materials.

This shop may be implemented with 'QShop'.

### Tutorial
As a tutorial, the player is locked in an office room and must scavenge chalk to perform a ritual which will unlock the first keycard to open the room as a quest reward.

## Endgoals

### Endpoint of Evolution
The player may create an 'ultimate' creature to serve them by capturing the required high-level mobs.

### Transhumanism
The player may seek to fully augment themselves with all possible augments.

## Relationship to Downfall Directive
Lusus Naturae can have a direct relation with the Downfall Directive Series as it shares many mods, concepts and themes.

Lusus Naturae shares the high technology and occult centered approach as several Downfall Directive packs such as Broken Protocol. The task force sent may be 'Dayfell', the Agency responsible for containing mirror corruption in Lusus Naturae, and many of the incidents in the facility may be caused by mirror corruption.
